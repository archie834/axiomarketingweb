17 Issues Found — On-Site & Off-Site

01 — Generic Title Tag

Rewrite to include services + Peterborough — see box above.

02 — No Meta Description

Google auto-generates a poor snippet from random page text instead.

03 — Zero Schema Markup

No LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Product or Review schema — invisible to AI search tools.

04 — No Address On Site

Phone appears often, but no street address/postcode anywhere — weakens local ranking (NAP).

05 — No Social Share Tags

No Open Graph/Twitter tags — broken previews on Facebook/WhatsApp.

06 — No Canonical Tags

Duplicate content risk via WordPress category/tag archives.

07 — Images Missing Alt Text

Product photos have no alt text — losing Google Image Search traffic.

08 — Duplicate Nav Code

Navigation menu repeated in the page code — bloat + crawler confusion.

09 — Generic H1

"Home & Living" has no location or service keyword.

10 — FAQ Not Marked Up

Easiest AEO win — wrap existing FAQ in FAQPage schema for AI citations & "People also ask".

11 — No "Near Me" Content

No mentions of Stamford, Huntingdon or nearby Cambridgeshire towns.

12 — 41 Years' History Untapped

Huge trust signal for customers and AI — currently just a passing mention.

13 — NAP Inconsistent

4 different addresses & 2 phone numbers across directories — see table below.

14 — Wrong Category Online

Peterborough Online lists the business as "Transport & Logistics".

15 — No Bing Places Listing

Invisible to Bing Maps & Microsoft Copilot — free 15-min fix.

16 — Reviews Scattered & Thin

0 reviews on 5+ directories; Facebook shows "not yet rated" (4 reviews).

17 — Duplicate Unmanaged Listings

6 directories carry separate listings with conflicting info.

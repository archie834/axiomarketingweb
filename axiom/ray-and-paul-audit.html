Ray & Paul Interiors

SEO / GEO / AEO Audit — rayandpaul.co.uk

Executive Summary

Ray & Paul has a strong real-world reputation — 41 years trading, family-run, leading home interiors supplier in the area — but the website isn't telling Google (or AI tools like ChatGPT/Google AI Overviews) any of that.

AreaScoreWhat it means
SEO (Google ranking)4/10Site is findable but missing the basics that help it rank for "blinds Peterborough", "curtains Peterborough" etc.
GEO (AI search visibility)3/10When someone asks ChatGPT/Gemini "best curtain shop in Peterborough", this site has almost nothing for AI to read and recommend.
AEO (Answer boxes / Featured Snippets)4/10Has a real FAQ section (great!) but it's not marked up so Google can't turn it into a rich answer box.

Headline issue: the page that shows up in Google search results currently reads:

Homepage - Ray & Paul

No mention of Peterborough, blinds, curtains, flooring, or what the business actually does. This is the single highest-impact fix available — it's the first thing every potential customer sees in search results.

Critical Issues (Fix First)

1. Title Tag Is Generic — "Homepage - Ray & Paul"

  • This is what shows as the blue clickable link in Google search results
  • Should be something like: "Blinds, Curtains & Flooring | Ray & Paul Interiors Peterborough"
  • Costs them clicks every single day — searchers can't tell what the business sells or where it's based

2. No Meta Description

  • Google currently auto-generates the snippet text under the title — usually a random chunk of page text
  • A written meta description directly improves click-through rate from search results

3. Zero Structured Data (Schema Markup)

None of the following are present:

  • LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema — tells Google this is a real local business with an address, phone, opening hours, services
  • FAQPage schema — would turn their existing FAQ section into rich "People also ask" style results
  • Product / Service schema for Blinds, Curtains, Flooring, Wallpaper categories
  • Organization schema with social profile links (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram)
  • AggregateRating / review schema — if they have Google reviews, none of that trust signal is on the website

This is the same gap that, when fixed, got Lucky Motors and Axio review-rich-results and FAQ rich results showing in Google.

4. No Address Anywhere on Site

  • Phone number (01733 775533) appears repeatedly — good
  • But no street address / postcode visible on the page
  • Google's local ranking algorithm relies on consistent Name, Address, Phone (NAP) across the website AND Google Business Profile — missing address = weaker local ranking signal

5. No Open Graph / Social Share Tags

  • When a link to rayandpaul.co.uk is shared on Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp, there's no custom title/image/description
  • Results in a blank or broken-looking preview — directly hurts social referral traffic

High Priority Issues

6. No Canonical Tags

Risk of duplicate content if the same page is reachable via multiple URLs (common on WordPress with category/tag archives)

7. Images Missing Alt Text

  • Product photos (curtains, blinds, flooring galleries) have no descriptive alt text
  • Missed opportunity for Google Image Search traffic — a real channel for an interiors business where people search visually

8. Duplicate Navigation Menus in Code

Page code includes the navigation twice — template/theme redundancy that adds unnecessary page weight and can confuse crawlers

9. H1 Heading Too Generic

  • Current H1: "Home & Living"
  • Should include location + service: e.g. "Made to Measure Curtains & Blinds in Peterborough"

AEO / GEO Opportunities (AI Search — the fastest-growing channel)

10. FAQ Section Exists But Isn't Marked Up

This is the easiest big win on the whole site. They already have:

"Common Questions About Home & Living" — covering measuring/fitting, samples, design advice

Wrapping this in FAQPage schema is often a same-day fix and can immediately make these answers eligible for:

  • Google "People also ask" boxes
  • AI Overviews
  • Direct citation by ChatGPT/Perplexity when someone asks about curtain fitting, sample ordering, etc. in Peterborough

11. No "Near Me" / Local Area Content

No mentions of surrounding areas (e.g. Stamford, Huntingdon, Cambridgeshire villages) that would capture broader local search intent

12. 41 Years of Trading History Is Untapped

  • This is a massive trust signal for both customers and AI tools — but it only appears as a passing mention in body copy
  • Should be in: the meta description, an "About" schema entry, and a dedicated trust-building section

Quick Wins — Recommended Action Plan (Priority Order)

  1. Rewrite the homepage title tag → "Blinds, Curtains & Flooring | Ray & Paul Interiors Peterborough"
  2. Add a meta description mentioning Peterborough, 41 years' experience, and core services
  3. Add LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with full NAP, opening hours, and social links
  4. Add FAQPage schema to the existing FAQ section (fast win for AI visibility)
  5. Add full address + postcode to the footer of every page
  6. Add Open Graph + Twitter Card tags for social sharing
  7. Add alt text to all product/gallery images
  8. Fix H1s on key pages to include "Peterborough" + service name
  9. Add canonical tags to every page
  10. Add AggregateRating schema once review numbers are confirmed from their Google Business Profile
  11. Clean up duplicate navigation markup in the theme template
  12. Add local-area content (Stamford, Huntingdon, surrounding Cambridgeshire towns)

Local Citations, Google Business Profile & Bing Places Review

Why this matters: Google can't confidently confirm where the business is, which directly weakens local pack rankings (the map + 3 listings that show for "curtains near me" type searches). This is likely a bigger ranking blocker than anything on the website itself.

14. Peterborough Online Listing Has a Wrong Category

The Peterborough Online directory listing categorises Ray & Paul under "Transport and Logistics > Distribution Services" — completely wrong for a curtains/blinds/flooring showroom. This listing is actively sending the wrong signal to Google about what the business does.

15. No Bing Places Listing Found

A search for "Ray and Paul Interiors Peterborough" on Bing returns no local business panel at all — just unrelated results about the film "Ray" and Ray Charles. This means:

  • The business doesn't show up in Bing Maps or Bing local search
  • Microsoft Copilot (built on Bing's index) has nothing to draw on when recommending local interiors businesses
  • This is a completely free, ~15-minute setup (Bing Places for Business) that's currently leaving visibility on the table

16. Reviews Are Scattered and Thin

  • Facebook: "Not yet rated" (4 reviews, no average shown)
  • Yell, Houzz, Thomson Local, Big Red Directory, Peterborough Online: zero reviews on all of them
  • Google Business Profile: not confirmed/verified during this audit — needs checking directly by the business owner (requires being logged into the account)

41 years in business should mean a strong base of happy customers — but none of that trust is visible anywhere online yet. A simple "leave us a Google review" prompt (via QR code in-store or follow-up text/email) would likely move the needle fast.

17. Multiple Near-Duplicate, Unmanaged Listings

Several directories (Yell, Thomson Local, Houzz, Big Red Directory, Peterborough Online, rayandpaul.tel) all carry separate, unmanaged listings with conflicting details. Left unmanaged, these compound the NAP problem above — each one is a small vote for a different address.

Citation Fix Action Plan (Priority Order)

  1. Pick ONE canonical NAP (recommend using the address tied to the verified Google Business Profile) and use it everywhere, including the website footer
  2. Set up / verify Bing Places for Business — free, fast, and currently a total gap
  3. Correct the Peterborough Online listing — fix the wrong phone number and wrong category
  4. Claim/update Houzz, Thomson Local, Big Red Directory, and rayandpaul.tel listings to match the canonical NAP
  5. Confirm Google Business Profile is verified, has correct hours, address, and category, and is linked from the website
  6. Start a simple review-generation habit — ask happy customers to leave a Google review (also feeds the AggregateRating schema in the action plan above)

This audit covers the public website plus a review of major UK directories (Yell, Thomson Local, Houzz, Big Red Directory, Peterborough Online, Facebook, Bing) as of June 2026. Google Business Profile details should be confirmed directly by the business owner, as this requires account access.
